O periódico **Nature methods** publicou um estudo de alto impacto centrado em inovação computacional e ciências ômicas: *"Scalable electron tomography for connectomics"*, por Kuan AT, Phan S, Delgado KM, Kim KY, Mackey MR, et al..

### O Desafio e o Contexto Computacional
We demonstrate limited-tilt, serial-section electron tomography (ET), which can non-destructively map brain circuits over large 3D volumes and provide high-resolution, supramolecular details within subvolumes of interest. Here we show accelerated ET imaging of semi-thick sections ( >500 nm) from the mouse cerebellar cortex, with 3D resolution <15 nm and machine learning-based image augmentation, providing the capacity to resolve key features of neuronal circuits including chemical synapses, endocytic structures and gap junctions. Furthermore, we systematically assess how imaging parameters affect image quality and speed to enable connectomic-scale projects.

### O Pipeline, Algoritmo ou Abordagem Computacional
