O periódico **Nucleic acids research** publicou um estudo de alto impacto centrado em inovação computacional e ciências ômicas: *"DriverDBv5: a multi-omics integration database for cancer driver gene research"*, por Lai YL, Wang YD, Shen PC, Liu HC, Chen YH, et al..

### O Desafio e o Contexto Computacional
Cancer is driven by alterations across multiple molecular layers, but whether multi-omics drivers carry prognostic weight has received little systematic attention. DriverDBv5 (https://driverdb.bioinfomics.org/) extends DriverDB from driver identification to prognostic evaluation. It has been expanded to 187 cohorts, 43 862 patients, and 129 655 samples, with 154 cohorts from non-TCGA (The Cancer Genome Atlas) repositories reconciled by a generalized multi-omics barcode. Driver genes can be evaluated with Cox and mixture cure models, with machine learning on the full multi-omics feature matrix, and for the joint prognostic effect of alterations in two omics layers. The Customized Analysis module has grown from 4 to 10 tools for user-defined gene sets, subgroups, and covariates, and a human body map offers an anatomical entry point. DriverDBv5 thus reveals not only which alterations drive a tumor, but also which of them relate to patient outcome.

### O Pipeline, Algoritmo ou Abordagem Computacional
- Desenvolvimento de metodologia bioinformática avançada para integração de dados biológicos em larga escala.
- Estruturação de fluxos de análise com ênfase em reprodutibilidade, validação e mineração preditiva.
