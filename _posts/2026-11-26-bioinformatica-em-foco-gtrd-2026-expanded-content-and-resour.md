O periódico **Nucleic acids research** publicou um estudo de alto impacto centrado em inovação computacional e ciências ômicas: *"GTRD 2026: expanded content and resources for deep learning"*, por Kolmykov S, Yevshin I, Kulyashov M, Sharipov R, Pintus S, et al..

### O Desafio e o Contexto Computacional
Gene Transcription Regulation Database (GTRD; https://gtrd.biouml.org/) contains uniformly annotated and processed NGS data related to transcription regulation: ChIP-seq, ChIP-exo, DNase-seq, MNase-seq, ATAC-seq, and RNA-seq. The latest release significantly extends the number of available experiments. All new ChIP-seq data for human transcription factors (TFs) obtained by the Codebook Consortium were processed and annotated; GTRD now covers 85% of all known human TFs. We added new types of information related to transcription regulation: enhancer regions revealed from analysis of CAGE-seq data and single-cell ATAC-seq data. To facilitate the use of GTRD for building deep learning models, we provide direct access to the MySQL database where metadata are stored and have built profiles with single-nucleotide resolution for ChIP-seq and ATAC-seq data.

### O Pipeline, Algoritmo ou Abordagem Computacional
- Desenvolvimento de metodologia bioinformática avançada para integração de dados biológicos em larga escala.
- Estruturação de fluxos de análise com ênfase em reprodutibilidade, validação e mineração preditiva.
